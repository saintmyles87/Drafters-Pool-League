Match Scoring Rules

  • Ball Potted: 0.25 points per ball.
  • Frame Win: +5.00 points to winner.
  • Black Ball Loss: +1.00 point bonus to loser (if 7 balls potted).
  • Shutout Win (8–0): +1.00 point bonus to winner (0 balls potted by loser).
  • Foul on Black: Opponent automatically awarded 8 balls and frame win. Offending player keeps points for balls potted up to the foul.

League Tiebreaker Order

  1. Total Match Points
  2. Head-to-Head Result
  3. Most Wins
  4. Total Balls Difference
