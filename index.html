League Rules & Scoring

Round Robin Points System

  • Win: 5 points base + (0.25 pts per ball potted)
  • Loss: 0.25 pts per ball potted
  • Shutout Bonus: +1 point if you win and leave opponent on 0 balls
  • Loss on Black: +1 point if you lose while on the black ball (7 balls potted)

Overall Championship Points

Points awarded per tournament based on final overall position (post-playoffs):

  • 6 Players: 1st: 10pts, 2nd: 6pts, 3rd: 4pts, 4th: 3pts, 5th: 2pts, 6th: 1pt
  • 5 Players: 1st: 9pts, 2nd: 5pts, 3rd: 3pts, 4th: 2pts, 5th: 1pt
  • 4 Players: 1st: 7pts, 2nd: 4pts, 3rd: 2pts, 4th: 1pt
